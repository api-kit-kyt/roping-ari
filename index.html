Roping Ari

Ari is a small creature who travels along trolleybus wires on two long poles that grew from her horns. When a trolleybus is in her way on the same wires she goes over it: a swing, a release, a turn in the air, and her poles back onto the wires ahead of it. The move is named after the ninja rope of Worms Armageddon. Here is a game of it and the physics sketches it came from, all in side view and to scale.

The moves, as demos

Ten candidate rewards of the game, each a scripted run through the same physics with numbered stops at its key moments (tick "Stop at key moments" on the page, or step with , and .). First on the current build, where her tips are a mass on the wire (coils, hands, friction); then the same ten on the kinematic-tips build of 2026-09-23, kept as it was.

  • Spun down badge: the hoop, then opened, then a soft catch
  • Held it badge: a hard catch
  • Through the arc badge: arced between the wires, stayed on, and still passed the bus
  • Short-pole flight badge: the lifted gait held for 2 s at the shortest pole length
  • Hoop over the roof badge: curled into the hoop with her head above the trolleybus
  • Kiss badge: a catch with under 0.5 m/s of speed along the poles to absorb
  • Swoop line: REL → SPREAD → CATCH → LIFTED before the swing back
  • Swoop below the wire line: REL → SPREAD → CATCH → LIFTED with her head never above the wires: a wing hop in the space under them, no trolleybus in the way
  • Rolled up line: REL → CURL → OPEN → SPREAD → CATCH in one flight
  • One breath line: PULL → REL → CATCH → PASS in under 3 s
  • Full repertoire line: REL, CURL, OPEN, SPREAD, CATCH and LIFTED, in any order, within 6 s
