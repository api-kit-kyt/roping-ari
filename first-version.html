Roping Ari

Side view, to scale. Ari swings over a trolleybus on her two poles the way a worm ropes over a wall in Worms Armageddon, except that her anchors slide along the wire with her. The playback stops at each key moment and shows its numbers.

Point-mass sketch: a 4 kg body on poles whose tips move along the wire at a prescribed speed, a ballistic flight, a second swing after the catch. No lift, no pole bending, no wire sag; drag only while she flares. Cruise, brake and the final settle are blends, not solved. Numbers and track come from roping_numbers.py and roping_animation.py beside this page; the treatment is ari-roping.md.
